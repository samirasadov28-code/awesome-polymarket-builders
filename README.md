## Project Descriptions

### Trading Terminals

#### [Betmoar.fun](https://betmoar.fun)
Web-based Polymarket trading terminal offering powerful search, real-time news, and unique market insights. An advanced terminal designed to enhance user experience with comprehensive market insights, customizable search filters, and integrated trading within a sleek UI. Uniquely combines live breaking news with predictive market data.

**Key Features:**
- Profiles tool to identify and filter active wallets by assets, P&L, and volume
- Integrated trading panel with granular filters and market strength metrics
- Real-time breaking news terminal with browser notifications
- UMA integration showing disputed markets and whale voting behavior
- Advanced search with quick filters, tags, and watchlist management
- Top gains tracker identifying biggest winners/losers per market

---

#### [PolyTraderPro](https://polytraderpro.com)
Desktop trading app for Mac, Windows, and Linux that lets you trade on Polymarket up to 5x faster with zero lag, smart alerts, and multi-wallet support.

**Key Features:**
- Ultra-fast trading built directly on API calls with minimal overhead
- Unlimited wallet/account management with instant switching
- Supports Magic Link, MetaMask, and external wallets
- Price alerts with customizable notifications
- Maker order fill alerts across all added wallets
- Anti-misclick protection with confirmation prompts
- Split, merge, and convert shares functionality

---

#### [Polymtrade](https://polym.trade)
The first mobile trading terminal for Polymarket with AI-powered insights. Mobile-native interface optimized for smartphone users on iOS and Android.

**Key Features:**
- AI models trained on 55,000+ resolved markets for predictive analysis
- Self-custodial wallet integration with user-controlled transactions
- Zero gas fees on Polygon network
- One-click trade execution with integrated MoonPay top-up
- Multi-chain wallet support (Polygon and Solana)
- Real-time market data, advanced search, and dashboard analytics

---

#### [Stand.trade](https://stand.trade)
Track and copy trade Polymarket whales. Receive lightning-fast alerts when prediction market traders make high-conviction moves.

**Key Features:**
- Whale and trader tracking with copy trading functionality
- Real-time trade and news feed integration
- Personalized Discord alerts
- Market watchlists with multi-timeframe price tracking
- Portfolio and position monitoring dashboard
- Custom labels for followed traders

---

#### [Bullpen](https://bullpen.fi)
One app for all of crypto. Trading terminal for onchain markets integrating perpetuals on Hyperliquid, spot assets, and prediction markets in a single interface.

**Key Features:**
- All prediction market trades execute through Polymarket's hybrid-decentralized orderbook
- No trading fees (same as Polymarket)
- Self-custody only with native bridging
- Available on desktop and mobile web app
- Earn across four ecosystems: Hyperliquid, Hyperunit, Polymarket, and Bullpen

---

### Telegram Trading Bots

#### [Polycule](https://polycule.trade)
Telegram bot for seamless Polymarket trading, enabling mobile, social, and group trades from anywhere in the world.

**Key Features:**
- Group social trading with automatic trade broadcasting in Telegram groups
- Mobile-first trading via simple chat commands
- Copy trading to automatically mimic successful users
- Wallet tracking with instant notifications
- Built-in Polygon wallet for deposits, withdrawals, and swaps
- Limit orders and real-time price notifications
- Group leaderboards and P&L cards for viral engagement
- Multi-level referral rewards (25%, 5%, 3%)

---

#### [PolyBot](https://polybot.trading)
Self-custodial Telegram trading bot for Polymarket featuring Gnosis Safe integration, gas-sponsored transactions, and seamless paste-to-trade functionality.

**Key Features:**
- Personal Gnosis Safe smart wallet for complete fund control
- Gas-free trading (no MATIC needed)
- Paste-to-trade: drop any Polymarket URL for instant market access
- Supports both USDC and USDC.e with automatic conversion
- Real-time portfolio tracking with P&L calculations
- Built-in market search with volume/category/trending filters
- 1% fee on successful trades; referrers earn 25%

---

#### [OkBet](https://tryokbet.com)
Telegram-based terminal for trading on Polymarket & Kalshi. Trade, bet, and copy top performers with friends, all from chat.

**Key Features:**
- Trade directly within Telegram without external websites
- Copy trading from leaderboards or any Polymarket wallet
- Group leaderboards tracking performance among members
- One-click trading links between OkBet and Polymtrade
- Simple 1% fee on buys and sells
- GCP KMS encryption for private key security
- Crossed $100M+ in volume; Polymarket grant recipient

### Funds & Investment

#### [PolyFund](https://polyfund.so)
Decentralized platform that enables skilled predictors to manage funds and investors to benefit from their expertise on Polymarket prediction markets.

**Key Features:**
- Decentralized fund creation with custom timelines and fee structures
- Permissionless access to public prediction portfolios
- Automated profit sharing (up to 3% deposit fee, 30% performance fee)
- Full on-chain transparency of trades and fund activity
- Account abstraction via email/social login (Privy + Zerodev)
- Real-time performance analytics and dashboards

### Aggregators

#### [Purebet](https://purebet.io)
The first on-chain sports betting aggregator. Aggregates odds and liquidity from 8 protocols across 6 blockchains ensuring users always get the best odds.

**Key Features:**
- Routes transactions to best venues (SX Bet, Overtime, etc.)
- Cross-chain integration (Ethereum, Polygon, Solana, BNB Chain)
- Non-custodial with no KYC or betting limits
- Never bans or limits winning players
- Orderbook format combining multiple protocol offerings
